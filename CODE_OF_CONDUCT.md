# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenVibe to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel of the project. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer, [@Mideky-hub](https://github.com/Mideky-hub), using the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue concerned.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the severity, the response may be:

1. **A correction:** a private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **A warning:** for a single serious incident or repeated behavior, with consequences if it continues. This includes no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **A temporary ban:** for a serious violation, no interaction with the community for a set period.
4. **A permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
